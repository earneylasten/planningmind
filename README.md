## What is PlanningMind?

The only tool that combines the 11-Dimension 
Destination Health Index with the 26-step 
Planning Blueprint Process (Lasten & Pizam, 2013).

Score your destination across 11 dimensions →
get your Butler lifecycle stage → see exactly 
which of the 26 planning steps apply to you.

## The 11 Dimensions

1. Destination Characteristics
2. Marketing Response
3. Economic Impacts
4. Social Impacts
5. Environmental Impacts
6. Housing & Labor Affordability
7. Climate & Resource Resilience
8. Cultural Authenticity & Language
9. Accessibility & Inclusive Tourism
10. Digital & Reputation Dynamics
11. Governance & Institutional Capacity
